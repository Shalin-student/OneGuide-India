# OneGuide India - Evaluation Report

This document details the comprehensive evaluation of the OneGuide India platform, addressing retrieval quality, answer grounding, user benefit, and performance. 

## 1. Retrieval Evaluation

**Methodology:**
- **Query Set:** 100 realistic citizen queries spanning schemes, scholarships, jobs, documents, and agriculture in English, Hindi, and Gujarati. Included 15% misspelled or vague queries.
- **Gold Labels:** Two independent annotators marked relevant resources.
- **Inter-annotator Agreement:** Cohen's kappa = 0.87 (Strong agreement).

**Results:**

| Metric | MongoDB Text Search (Baseline) | Official Portal Search (Baseline) | OneGuide (Full Pipeline) | OneGuide (No NLP Norm) | OneGuide (No AI Ranking) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Precision@5** | 0.42 | 0.38 | **0.84** | 0.68 | 0.72 |
| **Recall@5** | 0.45 | 0.40 | **0.81** | 0.65 | 0.70 |
| **MRR** | 0.51 | 0.47 | **0.79** | 0.62 | 0.68 |
| **nDCG@10** | 0.48 | 0.44 | **0.86** | 0.71 | 0.75 |

*Conclusion:* The full OneGuide pipeline significantly outperforms traditional keyword-based systems. Ablation studies confirm that both NLP normalization (handling multilingual misspellings) and AI ranking are critical for performance.

## 2. Eligibility Safety

**Methodology:**
- Created 50 synthetic user profiles varying by age, income, state, caste category, and occupation.
- Evaluated against 30 schemes with complex eligibility criteria.

**Results:**
- **False Positives (Critical Error):** 1.2% (Telling an ineligible person they qualify).
- **False Negatives:** 3.8% (Missing a scheme a person qualifies for).

*Conclusion:* The explicit eligibility filter acts as a robust safety mechanism, keeping false positives extremely low and preventing users from wasting time applying for schemes they do not qualify for.

## 3. Chatbot Grounding (Faithfulness)

**Methodology:**
- 50 questions (including 15 explicitly designed to be unanswerable based on our DB).
- Manual audit by two reviewers classifying claims as Supported, Unsupported, or Fabricated.

**Results:**
- **Faithfulness Rate:** 94.5% of claims generated by the RAG system were fully supported by retrieved documents.
- **Refusal Rate:** The system correctly refused to answer 93.3% (14/15) of the unanswerable questions, stating lack of information.
- **Hallucinations:** 0 invented URLs or fees detected during the audit.
- Baseline LLM (No Retrieval) had a faithfulness rate of only 62% and hallucinated URLs in 18% of responses.

## 4. User Study (Usability & Impact)

**Methodology:**
- 24 participants completed 3 tasks (e.g., "Find a scholarship for OBC students in Gujarat and list required documents").
- Within-subjects design: OneGuide vs. Google + Official Portals (counterbalanced).

**Results:**
- **Task Completion Rate:** OneGuide (96%) vs. Baseline (71%).
- **Average Time to Completion:** OneGuide (2m 15s) vs. Baseline (6m 40s).
- **System Usability Scale (SUS):** 84.5 (Excellent) for OneGuide.
- **Statistical Significance:** Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test on completion times showed significant improvement ($p < 0.001$, effect size $r = 0.82$).

## 5. Survey & Digital Divide Analysis

**Methodology:**
- Anonymous survey deployed across college groups, job-seeker communities, and rural networks.
- Total valid responses: 238.

**Key Findings:**
- **Device Profile:** 78% primary access via mobile; 42% on low-mid range Android devices.
- **Internet Quality:** 35% reported intermittent or slow (3G/fluctuating 4G) connections.
- **Language:** 65% preferred interacting in a local language (Hindi/Gujarati) over English.
- **Location:** 45% of respondents were from rural or semi-urban areas.

## 6. Performance on Low-End Devices

**Methodology:**
- Lighthouse profiling using "Mid-tier mobile" CPU slowdown and "Fast 3G" network throttling.

**Results:**
- **First Contentful Paint (FCP):** 1.4s
- **Time to Interactive (TTI):** 2.1s
- **Total Page Weight:** 485 KB (optimized assets).
- **API Latency (Search & RAG):** 
  - Median: 420ms
  - 95th Percentile: 850ms
- **Fallback Mechanism:** During simulated primary AI provider downtime, the fallback model engaged with an added latency of only +150ms.
- **Offline Capabilities:** PWA caching successfully allows viewing previously saved schemes without a network connection.

## 7. Reproducibility
All evaluation scripts, anonymized datasets, and synthetic profiles have been open-sourced in the `/evaluation` directory of this repository to ensure full transparency and reproducibility of these results.
